# drainage-report-build add-on: Town of Sahuarita

Load when: the Town of Sahuarita reviews the report, the site is in or will be annexed into Town limits, or the site is in
the Lee Moore Wash (LMW) basin. Load `addons/pima-county-rfcd.md` for County criteria, which apply only where the Town
confirms them in writing. QC tag: `sahuarita`.

## Governing manuals and studies (confirm current edition)
- Town code: floodplain (Title 14) and grading (Title 16). Only the table of contents is readable online; confirm content
  with Town staff rather than assuming County criteria apply (PHXZOO-004).
- Lee Moore Wash study hierarchy: PCRFCD 2018 supersession memo first; LMW-East TDN vs the 2008-2009 Basin Management Study;
  confirm the site is inside the governing study limit (PHXZOO-002).
- LMW Development Criteria (levee-like features discouraged; cutoff walls) (SAHPARK-019, SAHPARK-020).
- County criteria (RDM 2.10 roadway crossings, Detention / Retention Manual) as County criteria until the Town confirms
  (PHXZOO-004).
- Do not cite City of Tucson standards (SAHPARK-011).

## Methods
- Adopt published LMW flows per the supersession memo; state which engine's results are used where several are tabulated
  (PC-Hydro, HEC-HMS, FLO-2D) (PHXZOO-002).
- Design events: 10-yr and 100-yr; any other RP is a labelled sensitivity (SAHPARK-003).
- Q500 is not published; a 500-yr objective needs written client agreement to a ratio method or added scope (PHXZOO-002).
- New 2D models in the LMW: mesh 30 ft or finer (PHXZOO-002).
- Represent existing berm breakouts and flow splits; pre-ordinance berms cannot be compelled to be enlarged; sheet-flooding
  zone context (PHXZOO-002).
- Closed depressions (mine pits, tailings) bracketed contributing / non-contributing; agency agreement at the methodology
  meeting (SAH-016) (`hechms-headless`).
- Road alignment along a berm in a distributary area: compare straight vs berm-following (fill saving vs ponding, velocity,
  armouring, structure size); prefer the alignment that keeps existing drainage patterns; if the road acts as a berm, build
  mitigation to return flows to existing conditions; record berm owner and maintenance; size the drainage easement from
  headwater and setback before landowner negotiation (SAHPARK-019).
- FLO-2D road alternative screening per the screening-loop add-on of `flo2d-model-runs` (SAH).

## Criteria
- Roadway crossing (RDM 2.10, County criterion until confirmed): 10-yr contained, 100-yr overtopping 1 ft max
  (PHXZOO-004). A Town criterion of 1.0 ft max overtopping depth was recorded with its meeting date (SAHPARK-001).
- All-weather access: 12 in (Town); Pima County RDM: 1 ft in paved section (SAH-011).
- Cutoff walls per LMW Development Criteria (SAHPARK-020).
- Street drainage: 10-yr spread between curbs, clogging stated, scuppers in sump, ROW velocity < 3 fps; confirm which manual
  each value comes from (ELTORO-011).
- Depth exhibits: local depth ramp (D31) (SAHPARK-006).

## Review expectations
- Record each Town criterion with the meeting date it was agreed (SAHPARK-001).
- State Town-limits / annexation status, since it sets the floodplain authority; note any reversal by a later executed
  scope in the coordination record (PHXZOO-007).
- A designed overflow onto the site conflicts with matching existing peak Q and floodplain at the property boundary; needs
  Town and PCRFCD buy-in (PHXZOO-005).
- Meeting agendas list the governing agencies (Town, PCRFCD, county plant boundary where relevant) (PHXZOO-003).
- Hydrology appendix holds the LMW discharge tables used and an annotated location map (SAHPARK-011).

## Open questions to confirm
- Written Floodplain Administrator concurrence that a raised berm is a drainage training embankment in an easement, not a
  levee, before 60% (SAHPARK-019).
- Town adoption of RDM 2.10 roadway crossing criteria (PHXZOO-004).
- Whether FEMA effective / pending floodplains and PCRFCD 100-yr flows share one engineering basis (PHXZOO-002).
- ADEQ AZPDES CAFO permit applicability and 9 CFR 3.127 fencing at drainage penetrations for animal facilities (PHXZOO-004).
- Rise threshold for evaluation memos (D29): cite from the Town / PCRFCD; 0.10 ft was used once (ELTORO-018).
- Source of the 10-yr spread and 3 fps ROW velocity values (ELTORO-011).
