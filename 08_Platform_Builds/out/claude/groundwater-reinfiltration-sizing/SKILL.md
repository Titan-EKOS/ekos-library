# Groundwater displacement and reinfiltration sizing

Building cuts below perched or seasonal groundwater intercept seepage that must be collected (wall
and foundation drains) and returned to the ground. The design inputs move as architecture, cut depths
and geotech change: on the source project the calc was reworked 5 times in 5 months (HOMEWOOD-016).
Keep one dated workbook, run every revision through the same calc, and log what drove each revision.

## When to use
- Geotech reports perched or seasonal groundwater above the lowest floor, wall footing or under-slab
  elevation.
- A reinfiltration gallery (CMP, chambers, gravel trench) is proposed for foundation or wall-drain
  water.
- Any subsurface gallery is placed under pavement near groundwater (depth check alone).
Not for stormwater BMP sizing by itself; where the gallery also takes surface runoff, size both and
apply the jurisdiction stormwater rules (add-on).

## Inputs
- Geotech report and groundwater memo: perched water height, SHGW elevation at each facility
  footprint, permeability k (ft/day) per stratum, gradient basis, seepage area per face, infiltration
  rate at gallery depth (tested or untested), limits on infiltrating above lowest foundations,
  overflow requirement.
- Architecture: finished floors, lowest foundation and under-slab elevations, wall lengths and depths.
- Grading: cut depths, finished grade at the gallery, pavement structural section.
- Product data for the gallery (CMP diameter and storage per ft, chamber model, void ratios).
- Jurisdiction (load the add-on before any number is chosen).

## Steps
1. **Load the add-on** for the site jurisdiction (`addons/placer-county-ca.md` for East Placer /
   Tahoe). Write the governing SHGW separation and infiltration-rate basis at the top of the
   workbook with their source.
2. **Map cuts to groundwater.** From geotech get perched water height and seepage area; tabulate each
   wall or foundation face with cut depth vs SHGW / perched level. Faces fully above the water level
   carry no seepage; note them as zero with the reason.
3. **Seepage per impeded face (Darcy):** Q = k x i x A for each face (k ft/day, i ft/ft, A sf wetted
   face area below the water level). Sum the faces. State the gradient basis (geotech value or
   head/length) per face.
4. **Storage:** storage required = total Q x 24 hr (or the storage duration geotech or the agency
   sets). Gross gallery volume = storage / void ratio (gravel about 0.40; prefab chambers 0.80-0.90
   per product data; CMP from the manufacturer calculator).
5. **Infiltration area and drawdown.**
   - Infiltration rate at gallery depth: field-tested rate with the geotech factor of safety. If not
     tested, state the basis used and that field testing at gallery depth is required before final
     design (D23 core rule). The add-on sets any jurisdiction method for the untested rate.
   - Infiltration area required = Q / design rate (continuous seepage must be infiltrated); size the
     gallery footprint from the geotech infiltration area and check it meets this.
   - Drawdown of full storage = storage / (infiltration capacity - continuous seepage).
   - **0 in/hr sensitivity:** with no infiltration all seepage goes to overflow. The overflow path
     must carry the continuous seepage flow; geotech requires overflow to be incorporated.
6. **Depth check (CMP or chamber gallery):** finished grade to SHGW required = unit height
   (diameter) + bedding (2 in typical) + minimum cover to **bottom of structural section** (12 in
   typical) + structural section + required separation to SHGW. Compare to the available depth at
   the gallery. If short: shallower/wider unit, smaller diameter with more length, relocate, or a
   variance where the jurisdiction allows.
7. **Routing and site layout.** Route foundation and wall drains on the upslope side to the facility
   (cleanouts at bends and the inlet); provide an overflow with a defined outlet (storm drain, curb,
   or surface path) that does not return water to the foundations; slope paving 2% away from
   buildings; check geotech limits on infiltrating above lowest foundations (setback or depth).
8. **Run the calc** for every revision:
   `"%HYDRO_PY%" scripts\gw_reinfiltration_calc.py <case>.json --out <case>_summary.csv`
   (container: `python3 scripts/gw_reinfiltration_calc.py ...`). The JSON holds every input; the
   summary lists seepage per face, storage, gross volume, infiltration area, drawdown, the 0 in/hr
   overflow flow and the depth stack, plus CHECK lines (exit 2 if any check is not met: volume,
   infiltration area, depth to SHGW, stated infiltration basis, cited separation basis, overflow
   path). Carry the summary values into the workbook; the script output stays in 40_QC as the
   independent check (D30).
9. **Workbook and reasoning note.** One dated workbook (`20_Working/Groundwater/<YYYYMMDD>_GW_Reinfiltration_Calc.xlsx`);
   prior versions moved to an Archive subfolder, never overwritten. A design-reasoning note records
   for each revision: date, driver (architecture, cuts, geotech, agency), inputs changed, result
   change. Product calculator printouts (Contech, StormTech) filed with the revision they support.
10. **Report / memo text** (via drainage-report-build or drainage-technical-memo): state the seepage
    basis, storage duration, infiltration rate and its basis (tested, or untested with the testing
    requirement), separation to SHGW with its rule, the overflow path, and the 0 in/hr sensitivity
    result. Completed tense; no open questions in the deliverable (open items go to the D44
    open-items document).

## Jurisdiction add-ons
- `addons/placer-county-ca.md`: East Placer / Lake Tahoe Basin / TRPA - 5 ft separation from BMP
  bottom to SHGW (D25), untested rate = half the NRCS rate with a 0 in/hr sensitivity (D23, to confirm
  with the County), TRPA 20-yr 1-hr volume where surface runoff also enters, East Placer manual
  naming, WQ depth flag. Load with
  `get_skill("groundwater-reinfiltration-sizing", resource="addons/placer-county-ca.md")`.
- Pima County RFCD sites: the untested-rate method is the required-rate method in
  `get_skill("drainage-report-build", resource="addons/pima-county-rfcd.md")` (depth x FS / drawdown,
  D23).
- Other jurisdictions: no add-on yet; state the basis used and get the separation rule in writing
  from the agency or geotech.

## Gotchas
- Calc reworked 5 times Apr-Aug as architecture, cuts and geotech changed; keep one dated workbook
  and a design-reasoning note (HOMEWOOD-016).
- Whether overflow is needed for GW reinfiltration was left open in markups; geotech says overflow
  must be incorporated - always provide it and show the 0 in/hr case (HOMEWOOD-016).
- Under pavement, cover is measured to the bottom of the structural section, not to finished grade
  (HOMEWOOD-017).
- Markup example (2 ft separation): 36 in + 2 + 12 + 12 in = 5.17 ft + 2 ft = 7.17 ft; 24 in system
  6.17 ft; 18 in system 5.67 ft. With the East Placer 5 ft rule these become 10.17, 9.17 and 8.67 ft
  (HOMEWOOD-017, HOMEWOOD-009).
- Team assumption of 2 ft separation for the groundwater reinfiltration CMP needed confirmation
  against the 5 ft rule; SHGW at the galleries was not documented (HOMEWOOD-009).
