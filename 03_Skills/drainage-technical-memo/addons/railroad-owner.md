# Add-on: railroad owner (crossing rise memo)

1. Railroad guidance evaluates rise against subgrade and structure elevations, not one pass/fail rise limit
   (CAMPBELL-024). Report WSEL against top of subgrade and low structure elevations at each affected location.
2. Rise thresholds for exceedance durations are the ones the owner asks for, cited in the memo (D29); CAMPBELL-024
   used 0.1, 0.5 and 1.0 ft.
3. Show bridge and barrel flows separately and the depth x velocity^2 hazard for both conditions (CAMPBELL-024).
4. A 0.1 ft rise is not a universal trigger for railroad crossings (SILVERBELL-016).
