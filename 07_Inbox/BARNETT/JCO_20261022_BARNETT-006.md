function: hydrology
trigger: Any report that carries both regional (offsite) and local (onsite) design
  flows.
steps:
- Define regional and local storms and their peak flows in the existing-conditions
  section.

- Write allowable velocity as the flow velocity permitted over a given lining or soil,
  not as the velocity of the soil.

  reviewer flagged in April even though the definitions existed earlier.
