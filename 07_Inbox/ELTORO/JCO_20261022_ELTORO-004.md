steps:
- Copy the accepted project to a new working copy; never edit the accepted model in
  place
- Merge terrain (USGS 1 m DEM + survey at crossings) into one GeoTIFF in the model
  CRS (here EPSG:2868, NAVD88 ft); check the seam
- Extend the 2D perimeter to cover the full contributing basin edge; regenerate ~30
  ft mesh with breaklines on road crown, wash, curbs and path
- Build SCS CN infiltration raster (desert brush HSG A treated as B, 20% density =
  CN 83) and burn impervious path and pavement at CN 99
- Write NOAA Atlas 14 upper-bound hyetographs to DSS (5-min increments, alternating
  block for 1-hr; also 3-hr) for 2/10/100-yr
- Compare 1-hr IDF storm vs SCS Type II 3-hr and use the critical storm per TECH-018
- Keep offsite inflow BCs only for areas outside the mesh (see ELTORO-001); extend
  the outflow BC at least 5 cells past the area of interest
- Build existing and proposed plans for each frequency; split work so input prep is
  scripted and the compute is run on the desktop
gotchas:
- Basin sliver extended about 1,000 ft west of the perimeter NW corner; perimeter
  was widened to capture it
- Roads omitted from the CN layer are slightly non-conservative locally; burn them
  from plans or county land use
- Adjacent accepted study used FLO-2D with SCS Type II 3-hr on a 10 ft grid; moving
  the model from 1-hr to 3-hr aligned better with precedent
- Manning n kept uniform 0.035 to match the accepted model; changing it breaks comparability
  with the accepted results
