- Condition 10-ft DEM with WhiteboxTools (least-cost breach, D8 pointer and accumulation,

- Place CPs by rule - IN (offsite inflow >= 5 ac), OUTR (exit to Rincon Creek exclusion),

- Build incremental and total watersheds, longest watercourses, segment slopes and
  land-use/soil inputs.
- Write PC-HYDRO 7.4 .pk6 JSON per CP < 1 sq mi with NOAA Atlas 14 upper 90% depths
  at each watershed centroid.
- Route CPs > 1 sq mi to HEC-HMS (see RKN-006).

  results stay consistent.
