title: Culvert crossing in a sediment-laden piedmont - outlet protection, sediment
  and scour checklist
type: skill
function: hydraulics-model-run
trigger: New box culvert crossing in a distributary piedmont with high erosion hazard
steps:
- Report outlet velocity and size outlet protection per HEC-14; at 9-13 fps a dumped
  riprap apron alone is not enough (wire-tied or dissipator)
- Set inlet and outlet channel inverts at or below the box invert; note sediment risk
  when the culvert is perpendicular to flow instead of aligned with it
- Design cutoff walls for 100-yr scour plus long-term degradation per local Development
  Criteria; plan general scour calcs (HEC-18, HEC-20) for final design
- State whether overtopping meets the 100-yr depth criterion with margin; check sensitivity
  to effective weir length
gotchas:
- Draft had no outlet protection or scour content; 100-yr overtopping criterion was
  met with no margin
- Shortest culvert alignment was perpendicular to flow - sediment maintenance may
  cancel the cost saving
