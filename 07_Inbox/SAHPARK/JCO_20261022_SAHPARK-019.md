title: Road alignment alongside a berm in a distributary flow area - decision and
  berm characterization
type: skill
function: jurisdiction-rules
trigger: Road extension that can either run straight or follow an existing private
  flood berm in a sheet-flow area
steps:
- Compare straight vs berm-following alignment - fill savings vs ponding against the
  road, higher velocities, erosion protection, larger structures
- Prefer the alignment that keeps existing drainage patterns so no-adverse-impact
  is easy to show at the downstream property and County boundary
- If the road acts as a berm, mitigation to return flows to existing conditions must
  be built with the road; record who owns the berm and its maintenance
- Get written Floodplain Administrator concurrence that a raised berm is a drainage
  training embankment in an easement, not a levee, before 60%
- Size the drainage easement from the headwater and setback before negotiating with
  the landowner
gotchas:
- Berm-following option saved about 10,000 CY of fill but shifted flood-protection
  responsibility and armoring of a road carrying about 6,000 cfs to the Town
- Lee Moore Wash Development Criteria discourage levee-like features; berm status
  became schedule-critical
