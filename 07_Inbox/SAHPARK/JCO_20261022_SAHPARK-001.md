id: SAHPARK-001
title: Technical drainage memo must state data, assumptions, constraints, decisions,
  criteria, methods, results and conclusions
type: qc_rule
function: report-assembly
trigger: Any preliminary or final drainage technical memorandum for a roadway or site
  design
steps:
- Open the purpose section by stating the memo documents the approach and analyses
  that support the drainage design, not the project description
- Give each element its own subsection - data sources, design criteria (table with
  the authority for each criterion), assumptions, constraints, decisions with date
  and who made them, methods, results, conclusions
- Attribute agency direction (e.g., Town 1.0 ft max overtopping depth) to the meeting
  date it was given
- Always end with Summary and Conclusions plus next steps for final design; never
  leave it as an empty heading
gotchas:
- Draft 3 purpose paragraph described the road project only; no criteria, assumptions
  or decisions were stated anywhere
- Summary and Conclusions heading was empty in the draft sent for QC
