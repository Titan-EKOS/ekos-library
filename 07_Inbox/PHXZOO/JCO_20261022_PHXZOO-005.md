function: hydraulics-model-run
trigger: Conceptual drainage for a zoo or safari park crossed by a large offsite wash
  on flat terrain
steps:
- Lay out offsite conveyance alternatives (east perimeter with berm repair, central
  corridor, west perimeter, terraced channel) and count road crossings for each
- Prefer one consolidated crossing over split flow with several crossings; compare
  culvert cells for 10-yr vs 100-yr capacity since the upgrade is often one extra
  cell
- Terraced channel - low-flow channel sized for the 10-yr, overbank terrace for the
  100-yr kept outside animal enclosures
- Get written client decisions on (a) 100-yr vs 500-yr high-and-dry for enclosures,
  (b) on-site runoff containment for animal waste, (c) acceptance of controlled flooding
  of landscape areas
- Onsite - design each exhibit as self-contained retention and percolation; build
  refuge mounds from pond excavation
- Check any grate or bar rack at fence crossings for headloss and debris, not only
  security
- Next deliverables after decisions - channel cross-sections per alternative, then
  main entry culvert sizing (HY-8 or CulvertMaster), detention volume for up to 3
  site portions
gotchas:
- A designed overflow onto the site creates a second floodplain path and conflicts
  with matching existing peak Q and floodplain at the property boundary; needs Town
  and PCRFCD buy-in
- Double chain-link fencing passes stormwater freely, which removed the pipe-escape
  concern only because no pipes leave the enclosures
